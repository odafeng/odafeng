### 🧑‍⚕️ About Me

Colorectal surgeon at **Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital**, Taiwan.  
I work on robotic and laparoscopic surgery for colorectal cancer, and am building an integrated **digital surgery platform** spanning preoperative risk prediction, intraoperative video intelligence, and postoperative patient-reported outcome monitoring.

> 高雄榮民總醫院大腸直腸外科主治醫師。臨床上以機器人及腹腔鏡微創手術為主，正在建構一個整合術前風險預測、術中影像智慧分析、與術後病人回報結果監測的數位手術平台。

### 🔬 Research Directions

| Domain | Focus | Output |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🎯 **Preoperative** | Multimodal surgical difficulty prediction — CT pelvimetry, body composition, clinical variables | [`ctpelvimetry`](https://pypi.org/project/ctpelvimetry/) on PyPI |
| 🎥 **Intraoperative** | Surgical video analytics & AI-supported decision making for robotic colorectal surgery | Workflow analysis, video-derived outcome prediction |
| 📱 **Postoperative** | Digital symptom monitoring & AI-assisted patient education | [Hemorrhoids_PostOp](https://github.com/odafeng/Hemorrhoids_PostOp) |
| 📊 **Outcomes** | RiSSA technique with RA-CUSUM learning curve; NMF-based surgical difficulty decomposition | Stage III CRC prognosis modeling |
